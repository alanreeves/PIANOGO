Salamander Grand Piano samples
==============================

The MP3 files in this directory are a subset of the "Salamander Grand Piano V3"
sample library (Steinway Model D), recorded and released by Alexander Holm.

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Required attribution: "Salamander Grand Piano" by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0.

Modifications made for this app:
- 30 of the 88 pitches were kept (every minor third, MIDI 21 to 108).
- The medium-soft layer (velocity 10) was used, converted to MP3 by the
  `@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity10` package (MIT, Jan Forst,
  https://github.com/darosh/samples-piano-mp3), which republishes the same
  samples under the original CC BY 3.0 terms.
- Each file was renamed to its MIDI note number, for example `A0v10.mp3`
  became `21.mp3`.

Notes not covered by a sample are played by pitching the nearest one up or down
by at most one and a half semitones.

The full CC BY 3.0 legal code is available at
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode.
